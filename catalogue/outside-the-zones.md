### Bike Lane Memorial Sculpture

- **ID:** TPAM-097
- **Layer:** Layer 4 – Temporal / Rotating
- **Location:** Tom Riley Park (parking lot) — 3216 Bloor Street West (nearest address), Etobicoke, Toronto, ON M8X 1Z4
- **Accessibility:** 
- **Artist / attribution:** Community members (individuals not named in coverage)
- **Verified:** no
- **Coordinates:** 43.647175, -79.520297
- **Source:** https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/toronto/fourth-arrest-bike-lane-removal-protest-toronto-9.7387669 ; https://cyclingmagazine.ca/sections/news/toronto-bike-lane-memorial-honours-32-people-killed-on-3-major-streets/

Pyramid of red-painted bicycles attached to a utility pole, erected overnight in October 2026 as a protest memorial during the provincial removal of protected bike lanes on Bloor Street West. Each bicycle represents one of 32 people killed on Bloor Street, Yonge Street and University Avenue between 2000 and 2020, before bike lanes were installed (21 pedestrians, 4 cyclists, 3 motorcyclists, 4 drivers).
