# Working notes — April 24, 2026 (archived)

Origin notes for the Toronto Public Art Map. The current definitions live in the top-level `README.md` and the data in `data/artworks.csv`. Conversational text from the original notes has been omitted; the useful ideas from it are kept below.

## How the project grew

1. Started as an outdoor, bike-accessible list of downtown works, grouped by five zones.
2. Converted to a CSV (Name, Zone, Type, Accessibility, Plaza/Courtyard, Address, GPS).
3. Extended with sub-zones and a four-layer classification (CSV 2.3), then written up as "Atlas v2.3".

## Ideas for expansion (from the original to-do)

**Civic statuary**
- Court buildings (Osgoode Hall, Old City Hall surroundings)
- The wider Queen's Park statuary field
- Union Station exterior sculptures and commemorative plaques
- St. Lawrence Hall / King East civic monuments

**Architectural-integrated art**
- Relief sculptures on financial district towers (Bay Street)
- Sculptural facades at institutions (AGO, ROM edges, U of T perimeter)
- Bridge-integrated works (Don River crossings, Riverside cluster)

**Infrastructure and memorial landscape**
- Waterfront Trail memorial nodes
- Ferry terminal and Harbourfront civic installations
- Parkettes (Cloud Gardens as the prototype)

**Layer 4 (temporal / rotating)** — optional.

**Tooling ideas:** export the CSV to Google My Maps, Strava routes or custom bike tours once GPS is filled in.
